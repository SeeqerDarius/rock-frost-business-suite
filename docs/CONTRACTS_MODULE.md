# Contract Management

**Status (2026-10-09):** Core (Increment 5a), lifecycle (Increment 5b), and integrations and reporting (Increment 5c) are implemented. Core: contracts, parties, private versioned documents, version history and comparison, templates, clause library, confidentiality, numbering, settings, dashboard, register, and search. Lifecycle: approval workflows, obligations and milestones, renewals, amendments, termination, acknowledgements and recorded signatures, notifications, reminders, approvals queue, obligations register, and calendar. Integrations and reporting: links to Accounting, Fleet, HR, and Projects records, planned billing schedules tied to Accounting invoices and bills, calculated risk scoring, and ten reports with CSV and Excel export. An external electronic signature provider and public listing remain (see "Roadmap").

## Module and entitlement

- Module key `contracts`, route prefix `/app/contracts`, permission prefix `contracts.`. It is a normal catalogue module, so platform operators can enable it, subscriptions can entitle it, and access is enforced server-side by `requireModuleAccess("contracts")` on every page and action, plus `canAccessModule` on route handlers. `actorFromTenant()` passes permissions through `hasPermission()`, so contract permissions are inert whenever the organization is not entitled.
- `publicListing: false`: Contracts is not yet marketed on public surfaces (homepage, `/modules`, public pricing, self-service signup, sitemap, login). The public module page, screenshot, and public price will be added when marketing assets exist. Setting `publicListing` to true (and adding `MODULE_SEO.contracts` and a screenshot) publishes it.
- Pricing (confirmed by the owner on 2026-10-08): GHS 449 per month, GHS 4,490 per year, 8 seats included, GHS 25 per extra seat (create-once seed, in production since Increment 5a; operators edit it at `/app/platform/subscriptions`). It appears on the public pricing page and self-service signup once Contracts is publicly listed.

## Permissions and roles

`contracts.view`, `contracts.create`, `contracts.update`, `contracts.delete` (archive and restore; contracts are never hard-deleted), `contracts.approve`, `contracts.terminate`, `contracts.renew` (reserved for lifecycle increments), `contracts.manage_templates`, `contracts.manage_clauses`, `contracts.view_financials`, `contracts.view_confidential`, `contracts.manage_settings`, `contracts.export`.

Seeded roles: Contract Manager (all), Contract Reviewer (view, approve, financials, export), Contract Viewer (view). Organization Owner and Admin hold all permissions. The shared Team directory at `/app/contracts/staff` manages these roles.

## Data model (migrations `20261007090000_contracts_core`, `20261008090000_contracts_lifecycle`, and `20261009090000_contracts_integrations`, additive)

`ContractSettings` (numbering format and prefix, yearly reset, expiry alert days, confidential access policy), `ContractCategory`, `ContractType`, `Contract`, `ContractParty`, `ContractDocument`, `FileContent`, `ContractVersion`, `ContractTemplate`, `ContractClause`, `ContractClauseLink`, `ContractAccessGrant`, `ContractLink` (cross-module relationships, used by the integration increment). Every table carries `organizationId`; all lookups are organization-scoped. Indexes cover organization with status, expiration, renewal, owner, counterparty, category, currency, and start date, plus a GIN index on tags. CHECK constraints guard value, date order, notice period, currency shape, version numbers, and single-target access grants.

Lifecycle tables: `ContractApprovalRule` and `ContractApprovalRuleStep` (rules and their ordered steps), `ContractApprovalRequest` and `ContractApprovalStep` (one approval round per submitted version, with steps copied from the rule so later rule edits never change a running request; a partial unique index allows only one pending round per contract), `ContractComment`, `ContractObligation`, `ContractMilestone`, `ContractAmendment`, `ContractRenewal`, `ContractSignature`, and `ContractReminder` (the delivery log that makes reminders idempotent). New contract columns: `renewalTermMonths`, `noticeGivenAt`, `terminationEffectiveDate`, `terminationReason`, `terminatedAt`. New settings: `allowSelfApproval` (default off) and `obligationReminderDays` (default 14, 7, 1). CHECK constraints guard single-target approvers, value thresholds paired with a currency, non-negative milestone amounts, amendment numbers, and the renewal term (1 to 600 months).

Integration tables and columns: `ContractBillingLine` (planned receivable or payable lines in the contract currency, with the linked invoice or bill once invoiced; CHECK constraints require a positive amount, a three-letter currency, an invoice only on receivable lines and a bill only on payable lines, and a document on every invoiced line) and `ContractSettings.riskWeights` and `riskValueThresholds` (JSON; null uses the defaults). Linked records use the existing `ContractLink` table.

## Behavior

- **Numbering:** `{PREFIX}/{YYYY}/{SEQ:6}` by default (for example `CTR/2026/000001`), configurable with `{PREFIX}`, `{YYYY}`, `{YY}`, `{MM}`, `{SEQ:n}`. Allocated under a per-organization advisory lock in the organization timezone, unique per organization, optional yearly reset, and skips numbers already used.
- **Versions:** creation is version 1; every change to a versioned field, and every status change, creates an append-only `ContractVersion` with a snapshot, changed fields, a change summary (long text recorded as "changed"), reason, actor, and time. Active contracts require a reason to edit. Every contract has an internal owner (the creator by default); leaving the owner blank on edit keeps the current owner. Users with permission compare any two versions field by field. Users without financial access never see financial values in details, lists, versions, or comparisons, and cannot change them.
- **Lifecycle:** Draft, submit for approval, approved, activate (needs a start date and, when an approval rule applies, a completed approval), cancel (also withdraws a pending approval), mark expired, renew, terminate, archive (from draft, cancelled, expired, or terminated), and restore (to the last non-archived status). Every status change is a new version. A contract under approval is locked: its terms, parties, and clauses cannot change until the request is decided or withdrawn. Changing an approved contract's terms, parties, or clauses returns it to draft, because the approval covered the earlier terms. Status changes are always made by a person; scheduled jobs only send reminders.
- **Approval rules:** configured in Contract Settings by `contracts.manage_settings`. A rule has optional conditions (minimum value with its currency, category, type, department, minimum risk level, governing jurisdiction, branch) and up to 10 ordered steps, each assigned to a member or a role. The first active rule by priority (lowest number first) whose conditions all match applies; a rule with no conditions matches every contract. Value thresholds compare only contracts in the same currency; currencies are never converted. Contracts matching no rule are activated directly. Rules are deactivated rather than deleted.
- **Approval workflow:** submitting a draft creates an approval round for that contract version and notifies the first step's approver (the named member, or every active member holding the role). Approvers need `contracts.approve` and must be the current step's approver. They approve, request changes, or reject (a reason is required for the last two); each decision is recorded on the step, as a comment, in the version history, and in the audit log. Approving the last step marks the contract approved; rejection or a change request returns it to draft and skips the remaining steps. By default the submitter cannot approve their own contract (setting `allowSelfApproval`). The submitter or a settings manager can withdraw a pending round. The current approver or a settings manager can reassign the current step to another member, optionally marked as an escalation, with a reason. Anyone who can see the contract can comment. The approver of the current step can open the contract while that step is pending, even if it is confidential or restricted; access ends when the step is decided. A decision is refused if the contract changed after submission.
- **Obligations and milestones:** obligations record what each side must do, with type, responsible side, owner, due date, and recurrence (monthly, quarterly, semi-annual, annual). Owners can start, complete, waive (with a reason), and reopen their obligations without contract edit permission; others need `contracts.update`. Completing or waiving a recurring obligation schedules the next occurrence unless it falls after the expiration date. Milestones record key dates, with an optional amount visible only with financial access, and are marked achieved, missed, or cancelled (with a note). The obligations register (`/app/contracts/obligations`) lists open, overdue, due in 30 days, completed, or all obligations across contracts the user can see, optionally only their own.
- **Renewals:** `contracts.renew` renews an active or expired contract to a later expiration date (proposed from the renewal term when set), optionally with a new value (financial access required), as a new version with a renewal record. Recording a decision not to renew sets the renewal type to no renewal and records any notice date.
- **Amendments:** changes to an approved or active contract are drafted as numbered amendments listing only the terms that change (title, value, currency, dates, notice period, renewal type and term, payment terms, billing frequency, governing law and jurisdiction, description, text). Financial terms need financial access. Applying an amendment needs `contracts.approve` and, unless self-approval is allowed, someone other than its author; it creates a new contract version labelled with the amendment. Draft amendments can be cancelled. Earlier versions are never rewritten.
- **Termination:** `contracts.terminate` terminates an active contract with a reason, notice date, and effective date. When the notice given is shorter than the notice period, the user must confirm the shortfall, which is recorded. Open obligations due after the effective date are waived and planned milestones after it are cancelled, each with a note. The contract becomes terminated immediately, with the effective date recorded.
- **Signatures and acknowledgements:** an internal acknowledgement asks a member to confirm in the application that they reviewed the contract (optionally a specific document); only that member can acknowledge or decline, and the audit entry records the contract version and document checksum. It is labelled as an acknowledgement, not an electronic signature. Signatures obtained on paper or in another tool are recorded with the signer, date, party, document, and evidence. No electronic signature provider is connected; the `PROVIDER` method is reserved for one.
- **Notifications and reminders:** in-app notifications go to approvers when a step needs them, to the submitter and owner on the outcome, to acknowledgement signers, and to the owner and creator on termination; each opens the relevant contract tab. The daily `/api/cron/contract-reminders` job (07:00 UTC, `CRON_SECRET`) sends, for every organization entitled to Contracts: expiry reminders for active contracts at the configured expiry alert days and once when an active contract passes its expiration date; notice-deadline reminders for auto-renewing and evergreen contracts; and obligation and milestone reminders at the configured reminder days and once when overdue. Reminders go to the obligation owner or contract owner, only while they are active members. Each reminder band is delivered once (recorded in `ContractReminder`), so reruns, overlapping runs, and late first runs never send duplicates or bursts.
- **Linked records:** a contract links to Accounting contacts, invoices, and bills; Fleet vehicles, drivers, owners, and Work and Pay agreements; HR employees; and projects. Links are relationships only and never change the linked record. Linking needs `contracts.update` and access to the target's module, and the target must belong to the same organization. Users without access to a module see that a link exists but not the record's name or details; invoice and bill amounts appear only with contract financial access. A document that marks a billing line as invoiced cannot be unlinked while the line relies on it.
- **Billing schedule:** users with `contracts.update` and financial access plan receivable (to invoice) or payable (to be billed) lines in the contract currency, either as a recurring plan (monthly, quarterly, six-monthly, or yearly from a first due date until the expiration date, or for a fixed number of periods up to 120 when there is none) or one line at a time. A plan never creates invoices, bills, or journal entries. Once Accounting has raised the document, linking an issued invoice (receivable) or bill (payable) in the same organization and currency marks the line invoiced and links the document to the contract; each document can settle only one line, and the document itself is not changed. Planned lines can be cancelled with a reason. The schedule shows planned and invoiced totals per direction, and the document's status and amount paid.
- **Calculated risk:** each open contract gets a 0 to 100 score from factors with configurable points (0 to 50 each): assigned risk high or critical, value at or above the high-value threshold for its own currency, missing required clauses, active without a primary document, active without a recorded signature, overdue obligations, expiring within 30 days, past expiry while active, a notice deadline within 30 days on an automatically renewing contract, and active with no expiration date. Under 20 is low, 20 to 44 medium, 45 to 69 high, and 70 or more critical. The score is shown on the contract overview and in the risk report as guidance next to the assigned risk level, which it never changes. Users without financial access see the value factor only as a financial factor. Closed contracts score zero.
- **Reports and export:** `/app/contracts/reports` runs the contract register, expiring contracts (30 to 365 days, with notice deadlines), renewal forecast (12 months by currency), open obligations, calculated risk, counterparty exposure (active value and open planned billing by counterparty and currency), planned billing (by due date), amendments, terminated contracts (with short notice), and approval performance (outcome and hours to a decision per round and step). Reports cover only contracts the user can see; financial columns are removed without financial access, and counterparty exposure and planned billing need it. Values are shown per currency and never added across currencies. `contracts.export` downloads CSV or Excel from `GET /api/contracts/reports/[report]` (up to 10,000 rows, formula-safe cells, no caching), and each export is audited. The page shows up to 500 rows.
- **Calendar and queues:** `/app/contracts/calendar` shows expirations, renewal dates, notice deadlines, open obligations, and planned milestones by month for approved and active contracts the user can see. `/app/contracts/approvals` lists the steps waiting for the user (directly or through their role) and acknowledgements requested from them.
- **Parties:** multiple parties with standard roles or a custom role, legal and contact details, identifiers, signatory, primary flag, and an optional link to an Accounting contact in the same organization.
- **Documents:** private storage (`src/lib/storage/private-files.ts`): bytes in `FileContent`, never a public URL, SHA-256 checksum stored and verified on download, content-signature check against the declared type, 4 MB limit (Vercel request body cap; upload uses the `POST /api/contracts/documents` route handler because Server Actions are capped at 2 MB, with a same-origin check). Re-uploading the same type and title creates a new version that supersedes the previous one; nothing is overwritten. Removal is a recorded soft removal; the bytes are kept. `GET /api/contracts/documents/[documentId]` re-checks session, entitlement, `contracts.view`, and confidentiality, returns `no-store` attachments, and audits each download. Another organization's document returns 404.
- **Confidentiality:** standard contracts are visible to anyone with `contracts.view`. Confidential and restricted contracts are visible only to the creator, owner, and explicit grants (user, role, or HR department). `contracts.view_confidential` opens confidential contracts only when the organization enables "confidential admin access" in settings (off by default), and never opens restricted contracts. Lists filter in the database with the same rule; detail, edit, documents, and downloads report hidden contracts as not found. Viewing a confidential contract is audited. Platform operators work in their own organization context and cannot reach tenant contracts.
- **Templates:** versioned by code; one active version per code (activating retires the previous). Variables such as `{{organization.name}}`, `{{counterparty.name}}`, `{{contract.number}}`, `{{contract.startDate}}`, `{{contract.endDate}}`, `{{contract.value}}`, `{{contract.currency}}`, `{{organization.address}}` are filled at drafting; only the context's own keys render. The contract stores the rendered text and the template version; later template versions never change it. Templates can be duplicated.
- **Clause library:** versioned clauses with name, category, text, jurisdiction, language, owner, effective date, status (draft, approved, retired), and usage (recommended, required, optional, restricted). Only approved clauses attach; restricted ones need `contracts.manage_clauses`. The contract page warns when required clauses are missing.
- **Search and register:** server-side, paginated (25 per page, up to 100) search across number, title, counterparty, department, jurisdiction, tags, party names, and owner, with filters for category, owner, currency, tag, clause code, and value range (financial users). Views: all, drafts, active, expiring (90 days), expired, terminated, archived.
- **Dashboard:** real counts by status, approvals waiting for the user, overdue obligations, obligations and milestones due in 30 days, expiring in 30/60/90 days, past expiry while active, upcoming auto-renewals, renewal decisions due, 12-month expiry trend, risk and department distribution, and (financial users) active value by currency (currencies are never summed together), by category, and top counterparties. A summary widget appears on the organization dashboard.
- **Audit:** created, viewed (confidential), updated, confidentiality changed, status changes, parties added and removed, documents uploaded, downloaded, and removed, clauses added and removed, access granted and revoked, templates and clauses created, versioned, activated, approved, and retired, settings changes, approval rules created and toggled, submissions, step decisions, approvals, rejections, change requests, withdrawals, reassignments and escalations, approval resets, comments, obligations and milestones created and updated, renewals and non-renewal decisions, amendments created, applied, and cancelled, terminations, acknowledgements requested, given, and declined, signatures recorded and cancelled, links added and removed, billing planned, lines added, cancelled, and invoiced, risk settings changed, and report exports, with actor, organization, IP, and user agent.
